# Phase C Worker #3 UX Checklist

## Scope

Review the Phase C customer and AI operations surfaces:

- `/review/phase-c`
- `/review/phase-c/workforce`
- `/review/phase-c/timeline`
- `/review/phase-c/communications`
- `/review/phase-c/customer-success`

## Checklist

- Information hierarchy: each page answers what is happening, what needs attention, and what the operator should do next before passive detail.
- State clarity: loading, refreshing, current, empty, partial, stale, delayed, unavailable, not connected, permission restricted, unknown, and error states are visually and verbally distinct.
- Mobile priority order: critical state and action-required content appear before passive reporting at 390px and 375px.
- Keyboard and focus: navigation, filters, lifecycle controls, deep links, and cards are reachable with visible focus.
- Touch targets: interactive controls remain at least 44px high on tablet and mobile.
- Truthful copy: unknown, unavailable, reported, estimated, delivered, read, and verified claims are never blurred together.
- Provenance visibility: sources, timestamps, verification, freshness, related objects, and deep links remain visible without flattening.
- Reduced motion: state comprehension does not depend on animation, and reduced-motion mode has no running ornamental motion.

## Non-Goals

- Do not approve production mounting.
- Do not approve live adapters.
- Do not approve live communication sends.
- Do not approve customer success health scores until a score contract exists.
