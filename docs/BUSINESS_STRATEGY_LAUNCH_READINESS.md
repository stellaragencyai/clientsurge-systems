## Sales Call Script Outline
1. Confirm the business, industry, lead sources, and monthly inquiry volume.
2. Identify the biggest leak: missed calls, slow form response, no nurture, no booking handoff, old leads, or reviews.
3. Map the smallest package that fixes the leak.
4. Explain setup fee, monthly fee, and what goes live first.
5. Close on the next concrete step: package selection, onboarding, or follow-up with owner decisions.
