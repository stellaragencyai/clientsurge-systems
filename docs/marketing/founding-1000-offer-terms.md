- Offer applies to the first 1,000 eligible paid ClientSurge Systems signups.
- 50% off for life applies to the base subscription price for the selected package.
- Discount remains active while the account stays in good standing and keeps the subscription active.
- Offer is not transferable between businesses or accounts.
- No setup fee applies to founding accounts that complete signup during the founding offer window.
- Add-ons, usage-based fees, pass-through provider fees, taxes, ad spend, and custom development are excluded unless separately stated in writing.

- ClientSurge may refuse fraudulent, duplicate, or abusive submissions.
