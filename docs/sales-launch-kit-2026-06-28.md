## 2. Proposal / Audit Template

### Audit Title
Free {{Industry}} Automation Audit for {{Business Name}}

### Audit Promise
We will identify where leads are leaking between your website, phone calls, follow-up, and booking process, then show the fastest automation path to recover them.

### Sections

#### 1. Business Snapshot
- Business name
- Website
- Industry
- Location/service area
- Primary contact
- Main conversion goal

#### 2. Lead Capture Review
Questions:
- Is there a clear call-to-action above the fold?
- Is the phone number clickable on mobile?
- Is the form easy to complete?
- Does the site explain what happens after submission?
- Are trust signals visible?

Score:
- 0 = broken/missing
- 1 = present but weak
- 2 = good
- 3 = strong

#### 3. Missed Call / Speed-to-Lead Review
Questions:
- What happens after a missed call?
- Is there automatic SMS follow-up?
- Is the message personalized?
- Does it ask a clear next-step question?
- Does it include opt-out language?

#### 4. Follow-Up Review
Questions:
- Is there follow-up after the first response?
- Are email and SMS both used where appropriate?
- Are leads routed by urgency?
- Are inactive leads reactivated?

#### 5. Booking Handoff Review
Questions:
- Can leads book without waiting on staff?
- Is there a clear booking link/process?
- Are confirmations sent?
- Is there a reminder/prep message?

#### 6. Recommended Fixes
Rank fixes by speed and ROI:
1. Instant lead response
2. Missed-call text-back
3. Booking handoff
4. 14-day nurture
5. Review/request or reactivation

#### 7. Recommended Package
- Starter: response + missed-call recovery
- Growth: response + missed-call + nurture + booking
- Pro: full response, nurture, booking, reactivation, review, and priority setup

#### 8. Close
"Based on what I found, I would start with {{recommended_package}} because {{reason}}. The goal is to recover leads you are already paying to generate before spending more on ads."

## 6. Sales Call Script + Objection Handling

### Sales Call Objective
Diagnose whether the business has a real lead-response/booking problem and recommend the smallest ClientSurge package that solves it.

### Call Flow
1. Confirm context
2. Ask about current lead sources
3. Ask what happens after a website form
4. Ask what happens after a missed call
5. Ask how quickly follow-up happens
6. Ask how booking is handled
7. Quantify the value of one recovered lead
8. Recommend a package
9. Close next step

### Discovery Questions
- Where do most leads come from right now?
- How many leads do you think you miss per month?
- What happens if someone calls after hours?
- Who follows up with web forms?
- How fast does the team usually respond?
- Do you have automated SMS or email follow-up?
- What is one new customer worth?
- What would one or two extra booked jobs per month be worth?

### Recommendation Language
"Based on what you told me, the first problem is not getting more traffic. It is recovering more of the leads you already have. I would start with {{package}} because it fixes {{specific_problem}} without overbuilding."

## 7. Objection Handling

### Objection: "We already have a website."
Response:
"That is good. This is not just a website replacement. The question is what happens after someone calls, submits a form, or leaves without booking. ClientSurge works on the response and follow-up layer."

### Objection: "We already have someone answering calls."
Response:
"Perfect. This supports them. The biggest leakage usually happens when they are busy, closed, on another call, or the lead needs follow-up after the first touch."

### Objection: "We use a CRM."
Response:
"That helps. The question is whether the CRM is automatically responding, texting missed callers, sending reminders, and pushing people toward booking. If it is not, we can usually plug into the process rather than replace it."

### Objection: "Too expensive."
Response:
"Totally fair. The way I look at it is: what is one booked customer worth? If the system recovers even one or two jobs that were already slipping through, it can pay for itself quickly. That is why I recommend starting with the smallest package that solves the main leak."

### Objection: "We need to think about it."
Response:
"Makes sense. What part are you unsure about — the need, the timing, the price, or whether it will work for your business?"

### Objection: "Send me info."
Response:
"I can. I’ll send the short version, but the better move is for me to send the audit findings with the exact lead leaks I noticed. That way it is specific to your business, not generic."

## 8. First Outreach Rules

Do not begin high-volume outreach until:
- booking flow proof is clean
- checkout/payment proof is clean
- missed-call SMS proof is clean
- support/refund/onboarding SOPs exist

Allowed before full proof:
- manual relationship outreach
- soft audit offers
- low-volume prospect research
- script refinement
- proposal template creation

Do not promise:
- guaranteed revenue
- guaranteed lead volume
- guaranteed bookings
- legal compliance handled for customer
- full autonomous AI without human oversight
