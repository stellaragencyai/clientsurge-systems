# Free Automation Audit Call Script

## Opening
"Hey, this is Nolan with ClientSurge. Appreciate you taking the time. The goal is simple: figure out where leads are leaking, whether automation would actually help, and what I would build first if this were my business."

## Agenda Setting
"I will ask about your lead sources, response process, booking handoff, follow-up, and old leads. Then I will tell you the best-fit package, or I will tell you if I do not think this is worth buying right now."

## Qualification Questions
- What kind of business are you running?
- How many new leads or calls do you usually get each month?
- Where do most leads come from: website, Google Business Profile, ads, referrals, social, or old lists?
- What happens when nobody answers the phone?
- How fast do form leads get a real response?
- Who follows up when someone does not book right away?
- What tool or CRM do you use today?

## Industry-Specific Questions
### Roofing
- How many inspection or estimate requests come in during a normal month?
- Do storm leads get a different response path?
- How often do estimates go cold after the first visit?

### HVAC
- What happens during peak season when techs are already on jobs?
- How many missed calls turn into lost service calls?
- Do old estimates get followed up before seasonal demand hits?

### Dental
- How quickly does the front desk respond to new patient inquiries?
- What happens to treatment-plan leads that do not book immediately?
- Do recall or no-show follow-ups happen manually?

### Med Spa
- How fast do Botox, filler, laser, or consultation inquiries get answered?
- Do prospects get nurtured if they ask about price but do not book?
- Are review requests sent after positive appointments?

### Plumbing
- What happens when emergency callers cannot reach you live?
- Do estimate leads get followed up after the first quote?
- Which calls need immediate human escalation?

## Pain Discovery
"Where do you feel the money is leaking most: slow replies, missed calls, no follow-up, weak booking handoff, old leads, or reviews?"

"If we fixed only one part in the next 30 days, which one would actually matter?"

## Current Process Audit
Walk through one recent lead from first contact to final outcome. Note response time, channel, owner, status tracking, next action, and whether follow-up stopped correctly.

## Automation Fit Assessment
- Starter fit: response speed and missed calls are the main leak.
- Growth fit: response, nurture, and booking handoff all need coverage.
- Pro fit: full follow-up, reactivation, and review flow matter now.

## Pricing Transition
"Based on what you told me, I would not start with everything unless the leak justifies it. The package I would recommend is [Starter/Growth/Pro]. Setup is [amount], monthly is [amount], and the reason is [one-sentence reason]."

## Close / Next Step
"If that direction feels right, the next step is onboarding. We collect the business details, message tone, booking path, and provider access needed to build it. If you want to pause, I will send the summary and the decision points."

## Follow-Up If Not Ready
"Totally fine. I will send a short recap: the leak we found, the package I would start with, the price, and what would need to be true for this to pay for itself."

## Objection Handling
### "Too expensive"
"I get it. I would measure it against one recovered job, consult, or patient. If that math does not work for your business, I would not push it."

### "I already have a website"
"Good. The audit is about what happens after someone hits the website. If leads are already handled fast and followed up well, you may not need us."

### "I need to think"
"Makes sense. What I do not want is vague pressure. I will send the exact recommendation and the decision you need to make."

### "I already have a CRM"
"That can make this easier. The question is whether the CRM is actually triggering fast response, follow-up, booking, and visibility."

### "AI sounds risky"
"Agreed, if it is uncontrolled. The launch-safe version uses approved templates, clear rules, and human handoff for sensitive or unusual situations."

### "Send me info"
"I can. Before I do, based on what you told me, I would send the [Starter/Growth/Pro] summary. That way you are not comparing packages that do not fit."
