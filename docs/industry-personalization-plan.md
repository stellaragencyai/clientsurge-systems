# Industry Personalization Plan

## Goal
Turn the six homepage industry cards into a real industry-selection system that:

- makes each visitor feel seen
- recommends a tailored automation stack for their niche
- stays honest about what is deployable now vs consultative
- carries that industry preference into the rest of the website

This plan is designed to work with the current ClientSurge product catalog and current homepage industry section.

## Research Summary

These findings are what the industry recommendations below are built on.

### Med spas and aesthetics

- Boulevard reported that medspas using its platform improved online booking by 28% and reduced no-shows by 14% on average in the first year.
- Zenoti's 2024 medspa survey says almost two thirds of customers will not consider a medspa unless it has a rating of 4.5 or higher.
- Zenoti also frames personal connection, digital convenience, and "no waiting around" as core current medspa expectations.

### Dental and orthodontics

- The ADA says some practices lose 30-50% of initial prospective patient contacts and specifically recommends missed-call text messages for missed new-patient calls.
- ADA polling has repeatedly shown that roughly 8 in 10 dentists say short-notice cancellations and no-shows are a major reason schedules are not full.

### Chiropractic and physical therapy

- APTA's 2024 outpatient benchmark says vacancy rates in outpatient PT practices are 9.5%, with about 13% of PT/PTA positions open.
- Recent PT research notes outpatient no-show or cancellation rates can range widely, often 10% to 73%, and identifies access, perceived value, and follow-through as real reasons patients drop off after evaluation.

### HVAC, plumbing, and home services

- Valve+Meter's home services speed-to-lead study found the odds of connecting with a lead increase 21x when calling back within five minutes versus 30 minutes.
- That same study showed the slowest home-services response time in their sample was 5 days, 19 hours, and 9 minutes.
- ServiceTitan published a 2025 case study where better call handling increased call booking rates by nearly 13%.

### Roofing and restoration

- Roofing Contractor's 2024 homeowner survey found 73% of homeowners start with word of mouth, 54% also use search, and 97% expect a response from a roofer within a week.
- More than half of homeowners in that survey preferred a one-to-two-day response window.

### Contractors and trades broadly

- EZ Texting's 2024 consumer report says 42% of consumers expect a business response within 15 minutes and 66% within one hour when texting a business.
- BrightLocal's 2024 review survey says consumers are 41% more likely to use a business that responds to all reviews, and 93% expect review responses at all.

## Suggested Personalized UX Flow

## Click behavior

When a visitor clicks one of the six industry cards:

1. Set `activeIndustry`
2. Keep the industry card section visible
3. Expand or swap in a recommendation panel directly below the cards
4. Show the industry's recommended stack
5. Explain why that stack fits this niche
6. Offer one primary CTA and one secondary CTA

## Recommendation panel content

Each industry's panel should show:

- Industry headline
- One-line "this is what businesses in this niche usually lose"
- Recommended package badge
- 4-6 service cards in recommended order
- A short "why this works for your niche" explanation
- Optional consultative add-ons
- CTA buttons:
  - `See recommended stack in AI Store`
  - `Book demo for this industry`

## Personalization handoff across the site

Once someone chooses an industry, we should persist it through:

- `localStorage`
- query param for sharable links
- hidden form field in demo booking
- store preselection or recommendation rail

Recommended data keys:

- `selected_industry_key`
- `recommended_package_key`
- `recommended_service_keys`

## Safe Personalization Rules

Do this:

- change copy, examples, proof points, and recommended services
- preselect the recommended package in the store
- prefill the demo form with industry
- carry the industry choice into CTA language

Do not do this:

- claim a stack is live for an industry if it is only consultative
- imply a niche has a dedicated backend if it does not
- auto-hide the full catalog
- invent fake industry performance numbers

## Step-by-Step Implementation Program

## Phase 1: Content and data structure

1. Create one canonical `INDUSTRY_RECOMMENDATIONS` data object for all six industries.
2. Include: title, problems, recommended package, deployable services, consultative add-ons, explanation, CTA labels, and proof points.
3. Replace the current stale modal copy with this new data model.

## Phase 2: Homepage interaction

4. Update the homepage industry section so all six cards trigger the same recommendation behavior.
5. Render a dynamic recommendation panel below the grid instead of a modal-only experience.
6. Show the industry-specific recommended stack and the "why this works" explanation immediately after click.

## Phase 3: Store and CTA tailoring

7. When the user clicks through to the store, preload the recommended package or services for that industry.
8. When the user clicks demo, attach the selected industry and recommended stack to the form submission.

## Phase 4: Consistency and truthfulness

9. Add explicit labels:
   - `Recommended for your industry`
   - `Available now`
   - `Available by review`
10. Keep the catalog source of truth tied to the real service inventory so personalization never drifts from what is actually sold.

## Phase 5: Measurement

11. Track industry-card clicks, recommendation-panel CTA clicks, and package selection by industry.
12. Measure which industries drive demo requests, store visits, checkout starts, and booked calls.

## Recommended Copy Structure For The Panel

Use this exact order:

1. `For [industry], this is the stack we recommend first.`
2. `Why: [one sentence about the biggest operational leak]`
3. `Your recommended system`
4. service cards with plain-English explanations
5. `Add-ons for this industry`
6. CTA

## Ideas Worth Considering

### Idea 1: Industry memory

Once a visitor picks an industry, keep the rest of the site lightly personalized for the rest of that session.

Examples:

- homepage CTA becomes `See the best setup for med spas`
- store banner becomes `Recommended stack for dental practices`
- book page headline becomes `Let's map the right automation stack for your practice`

### Idea 2: "Why this stack" mini-proof strip

Under each industry's recommendation panel, show three short reasons:

- answers leads faster
- protects booked revenue
- builds trust and repeat business

### Idea 3: Honest availability labeling

Show two rails when needed:

- `Deployable now`
- `Advanced add-ons by review`

That gives the industry-specific feeling without overpromising deliverability.

### Idea 4: Industry-specific recommended default package

Default each industry to one package first:

- Med Spa: Pro System
- Dental: Growth System
- Chiropractic/PT: Pro System
- HVAC/Plumbing: Growth System
- Roofing: Pro System
- Contractors/Trades: Growth System

### Idea 5: Expand later into dedicated landing pages

After the shared recommendation system works, the highest-value next step would be dedicated pages for:
