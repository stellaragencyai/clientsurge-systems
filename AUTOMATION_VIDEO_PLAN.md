# Automation Video Plan

## Goal
Create one detailed demo video that explains the automation benefits clearly enough to improve trust, shorten sales cycles, and support homepage conversion.

## Recommended Format
1. One flagship video: 4 to 7 minutes
2. Two short cutdowns: 30 to 60 seconds each
3. One silent captioned clip for social or landing-page embed

## Primary Audience
Appointment-based businesses that already get leads but lose bookings due to slow response, missed calls, and weak follow-up.

## Core Promise
Show exactly how ClientSurge turns inbound interest into booked appointments without requiring more staff follow-up.

## Detailed Video Outline
1. Problem setup
   Explain how leads are lost in the first few minutes.
2. The old workflow
   Show missed calls, delayed replies, and manual follow-up gaps.
3. The new workflow
   Show one lead moving through:
   - instant response
   - missed-call text-back
   - follow-up sequence
   - booking handoff
4. Admin visibility
   Show the lead pipeline and notification history.
5. Business outcomes
   Explain time saved, missed leads recovered, and more booked appointments.
6. Implementation
   Explain that the system is installed for the client and launched quickly.
7. CTA
   Invite the viewer to book a tailored demo.

## Screen Recording Checklist
1. Homepage CTA to booking flow
2. Demo booking form
3. Example SMS confirmation
4. Example email confirmation
5. Admin lead detail view
6. Integration Health or event visibility
7. Pricing or offer summary

## Script Structure
1. Hook:
   “Most businesses do not need more leads. They need faster follow-up.”
2. Problem:
   “When a lead waits, intent drops and competitors win.”
3. Solution:
   “ClientSurge automates first response, missed-call recovery, and follow-up.”
4. Walkthrough:
   “Here is exactly what happens from inquiry to booking.”
5. Outcome:
   “Your team spends less time chasing and more time closing.”
6. CTA:
   “Book a free demo and we’ll map this to your business.”

## Best Placement
1. Homepage proof or how-it-works section
2. `/med-spa`
3. Demo follow-up emails
4. Sales follow-up messages

## Production Notes
1. Use real product/process footage before making a polished brand film.
2. Add captions from day one.
3. Keep the visual story operational, not abstract.
4. Reuse the flagship video to create short clips later.
